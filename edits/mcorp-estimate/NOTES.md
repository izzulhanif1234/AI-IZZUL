1:56.5, 1920x1080, HyperFrames project in `hf/`. Scenes are sub-compositions in `hf/compositions/`;
`hf/index.html` is generated by `scripts/mcorp_index.py`; subtitles by `scripts/mcorp_captions.py`
from the cue list built by `scripts/mcorp_timeline.py` (single source of timings).

## Audio
- VO: Microsoft Edge neural TTS, voice `ms-MY-OsmanNeural` (male, Malaysian Malay), script from
  `MCORP EstiMATE 13.10.pdf`. Lines in `hf/assets/vo/` with word timings (`.json`).
- Music: two MusicGen-small tracks generated offline (A: cinematic piano/strings, B: driving
  trailer), A for 0-66 s, B from the impact scene, ducked under every VO line and dialogue cut.
- SFX: bundled Pixabay library (`hf/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
- Dialogue clips loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS; final export normalised to -14 LUFS.
