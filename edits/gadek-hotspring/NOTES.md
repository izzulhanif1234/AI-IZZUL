# Gadek Hotspring Wellness & Spa Resort — Video Korporat (16:9)

2:17 (137.0 s), 1920x1080, HyperFrames project in `hf/`. `hf/index.html` is generated by
`scripts/gadek_build.py` (footage cuts, VO, SFX, music ducking: single source of timings).
Graphics are hand-written in `fx.tpl.html` (edit times); the build writes `hf/compositions/fx.html`.

Facts on screen and in the VO come only from https://www.gadekhotspring.my (Sejarah Kami,
Aktiviti/Fasiliti, Jenis Bilik, Tarikan, Polisi, Promosi pages, read 18 Nov 2026).

## Audio
- VO: Google Gemini TTS, voice "Charon", `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` with the prompt prefix
  "Say in a warm, calm and confident corporate documentary narrator voice: " (v01–v12);
  v13 from `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (free-tier daily quota of 10 requests on 2.5 flash ran out).
  Lines trimmed of silence and loudnorm −16 LUFS → `hf/assets/vo/*.wav`; whisper-small word timings
  in `*.wx.json`. `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` reads style prefixes aloud and rejects systemInstruction,
  so it is not used. The key is read from a local file (`GEMINI_KEY_FILE`), passed as `?key=`
  (the environment proxy overwrites the `x-goog-api-key` header with an old, invalid key).
- Timing: the edit was laid out against Edge TTS guide takes (`hf/assets/vo_edge/`).
  `scripts/gadek_build.py` maps every edit time (cuts, SFX, graphics) to film time through a
  piecewise-linear warp anchored on each word shared by the Edge and Gemini takes, so pop-ins still
  land on their spoken word. `fx.tpl.html` → `compositions/fx.html` gets the anchors.
- Music: MusicGen-small (`scripts/musicgen_long.py`), two 60 s generations whose continuation broke
  after ~50 s; bed = A 0–50 + B 0–46 + A 2–50 with 3 s crossfades, loudnorm −16 LUFS,
  ducked to 0.16 under VO, faded at the end by automation.
- SFX: shared Pixabay library (`hf/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
