# Prompt video AI — 6 cabaran (babak doodle)

Untuk Veo / Google Flow / mana-mana penjana video AI. Setiap klip 4–5 saat, 16:9, 1080p, **tanpa teks dan tanpa logo** (teks dan nombor 01–06 akan ditambah dalam video). Gaya yang sama untuk keenam-enam klip: pejabat kerajaan Malaysia, cahaya lembut kehijauan, kamera bergerak perlahan, sinematik.

**Gaya umum** (tambah di hujung setiap prompt):
> cinematic, realistic, Malaysian government office, soft natural light with a subtle green tint, slow dolly camera movement, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no people's faces in focus, 16:9

1. **Penggunaan kertas tinggi**
   > Close-up of a large office printer continuously printing pages, a tall stack of paper growing on the tray, more reams of A4 paper boxes stacked beside it
2. **Ruang simpanan fizikal besar**
   > Slow dolly along a long, crowded archive room filled floor to ceiling with steel shelves of thick ring binders and cardboard file boxes
3. **Pertindihan data**
   > Two identical paper forms side by side on an office desk, a hand placing a third identical copy on top, overlapping stacks of duplicate documents
4. **Carian maklumat lambat**
   > Analog wall clock above a cluttered desk, the minute hand moving fast in time-lapse while piles of files remain unopened, a sense of waiting
5. **Risiko kehilangan dokumen**
   > A single paper document slipping off a messy pile of files and falling into a gap behind a filing cabinet, dust in a beam of light
6. **Kos operasi tinggi** (kos tenaga kerja)
   > Close, intimate shot of a single office desk in a small Malaysian government office, one staff member seen from behind, buried in tall stacks of paper files, manually stamping and signing forms one by one, a wall clock above showing the time racing in time-lapse, evening light through the window, slow push-in

   Alternatif:
   > Medium shot of two facing desks in a small office corner, two staff (faces not visible) passing the same thick paper file back and forth, checking and stamping, piles of forms on both desks, a wall clock behind them, late afternoon light, slow dolly

Bila dah jana, hantar fail video (MP4) dan aku ganti doodle dengan klip ni (nombor dan tajuk kekal).
